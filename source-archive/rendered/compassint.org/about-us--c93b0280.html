Introduction

Welcome to Compass International – Your Trusted Technology Partner.

Established in 2007 in Sharjah and now headquartered in Dubai, Compass International LLC is a leading IT company specializing in delivering business-driven technology solutions across the UAE and the Middle East. With over two decades of combined expertise, we empower organizations through scalable, cloud-based ERP systems, customized software solutions, and comprehensive IT services.

From our early beginnings, we've grown into a global presence with strategic branches in India (2012) for development, Egypt (2016) for outsourcing and support, and Canada (2017) for research and innovation. Our services go beyond software—we provide full lifecycle support, including training, IT manpower supply, outsourcing, maintenance, repair, and post-implementation services.

Our highly experienced teams work collaboratively with clients from various industries—ranging from startups to government entities—to drive digital transformation with clarity, efficiency, and long-term value. Whether you're seeking Oracle E-Business Suite consultancy, immersive Metaverse experiences, or advanced infrastructure integration, Compass is your partner in progress.

Let us help you turn technology into a strategic advantage.

Why Compass ?

At Compass International, we believe technology should empower growth, not complicate it. As a trusted IT partner based in Dubai, we bring clarity, direction, and innovation to every project—helping businesses navigate the complexities of digital transformation with confidence.

With a deep understanding of regional and global markets, we specialize in delivering tailored IT solutions that align with your unique goals. Whether it's enterprise software integration, cloud infrastructure, or custom development, our approach is always strategic, scalable, and future-ready.

What sets us apart is not just what we do—but how we do it. We combine technical expertise with a collaborative mindset, working closely with clients to create impact-driven outcomes. From startups to government entities, organizations choose Compass because we don’t just build solutions—we build partnerships that last.

Let us be your guide in a world of constant change.
